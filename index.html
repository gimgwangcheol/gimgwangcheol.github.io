Concept

드나듦  Deunaneum — a Korean noun built from the verbs to enter and to leave. A going back and forth with no destination, where the going itself is the state, and that state is the daily change.

The work is not a structure built to arrive at some point in figuration or abstraction, nor a re-examination of art history. It moves.

Statement

My view of society is existential. I lived through the 1980 military coup and the May 18 Gwangju Democratization Movement, in which many citizens were killed by soldiers of their own country. My school years passed under military dictatorship, so questions of personal freedom and democracy came to me early. I began reading Taoism, Buddhism, psychology, sociology, philosophy and history. Philosophy came first, but I entered an art department and began painting and performance art.

My way of seeing is multiple and dialectical. I recognise value in life itself, and I take myself as one balanced agent moving through society. My role is to open humanistic discourse by widening what artistic expression can do. Performance art suits this, because it moves through many forms at once — exhibition, presentation, festival, organising, archiving, writing — and because it keeps a solid physical world flexible by refusing to let thinking harden.

Alongside the work I have organised self-directed performance art events for over two decades, and founded a performance academy that ran in Gwangju, Seoul, Dhaka and Wanju.

I call myself 미술워커, a maker rather than an artist: someone who simply does the work.
